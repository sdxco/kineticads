Compliance

Creative your legal team does not kill.

Since 2023 the FTC holds the endorser liable, not only the advertiser, and it has acted against companies specifically for claims made across their affiliate networks. In health and finance that is the difference between a creative you can scale and one that gets a channel shut.

We write the constraint into the creative rather than bolting it on at review. The supplement cut above makes zero health claims — it sells entirely on label literacy.

  • Structure and sourcing claims
  • Price, format, delivery, guarantees
  • Disclosure built into the cut
  • Regulated-vertical review before delivery
  • No invented testimonials
  • No fabricated metrics or client names
  • No real person's likeness without consent
  • No casino, betting or adult work
